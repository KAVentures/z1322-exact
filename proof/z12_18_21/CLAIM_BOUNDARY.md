# Claim boundary

This package claims a computer-assisted proof of

- `Z(12,18;3,3) = 108`,
- `Z(12,19;3,3) = 114`,
- `Z(12,20;3,3) = 120`, and
- `Z(12,21;3,3) = 126`.

It also verifies the already reported value `Z(12,22;3,3)=132` and shows that all five values fit the single formula `Z(12,n;3,3)=6n` for `18 <= n <= 22`.

The lower-bound family is a truncation of an explicit classical `3-(12,6,2)` Hadamard design. The construction itself is not claimed as new. The claimed new ingredient is the exact upper bound at `(12,18)`, plus its deletion-propagated consequences at `(12,19)`, `(12,20)`, and `(12,21)`.

The proof has been replayed exactly by the included verifier but has not yet undergone independent expert review or peer review.
